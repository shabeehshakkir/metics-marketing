# Key Features
## For Buyers
- Create RFQs with headers, lines, attributes (open/closed bids, partial bidding, show bid prices before deadline).
- Invite authorized suppliers or open to all in category; receive multiple bids.
- Compare bids side-by-side; negotiate via chat; award best offer.
- Auto-generate Purchase Orders on award; export PO as PDF.
- Edit RFQs (pre-deadline), save drafts, import via CSV/API.
- Manage suppliers (contacts, docs, contracts) and team roles.

## For Suppliers
- Register, set company & categories; receive category-matched RFQs.
- Submit bids (including partial quantities), add T&C templates and remarks.
- Track bid status and buyer responses; accept/reject Purchase Orders.
- Maintain client list; publish shareable product catalog; handle inquiries.
- Store compliance documents; manage organization templates and settings.

## Collaboration & Control
- Real-time chat per RFQ, FAQs, notifications, and activity updates.
- Role-based access, auditability, and document/version control.
- Support for Incoterms and international trade contexts.

# Use Cases (Cards)
1) **General Contractor**
   - *Problem:* Fragmented tendering across trades creates delays and cost drift.
   - *Solution:* Issue RFQs per package (steel, MEP, finishes), collect comparable bids, award or split via partial awards, auto-PO.
   - *Outcome:* Faster award cycles, documented decisions, predictable delivery.

2) **Quantity Surveyor / Consultant**
   - *Problem:* Manual bid normalization and reporting wastes time.
   - *Solution:* Side-by-side comparisons, partial bidding support, automated tender summaries and supplier scorecards.
   - *Outcome:* Less spreadsheet work, clearer advice to clients, repeatable governance.

3) **Developer / Project Owner**
   - *Problem:* Limited visibility into supplier capacity and cost risk.
   - *Solution:* Transparent comparisons, partial awards across multiple suppliers, live updates on bids and POs.
   - *Outcome:* Lower risk of over-reliance, better budget adherence, traceable procurement.

4) **SME Supplier**
   - *Problem:* Hard to discover opportunities and prove reliability.
   - *Solution:* Category-matched RFQs, reusable T&C templates, public product catalogs with inquiry flow.
   - *Outcome:* More invitations, higher win rate, cleaner POs and communication.

5) **Enterprise Procurement**
   - *Problem:* High RFQ volume and compliance demands.
   - *Solution:* CSV/API import, controlled supplier lists, audit trails, PO PDFs, centralized chat & docs.
   - *Outcome:* Scalable operations, governance by design, ERP-friendly workflows.
