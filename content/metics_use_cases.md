# Use Case Library

## 1) General Contractor: Multi-Package Tendering
**Audience:** Tier-2/3 GC, regional builder.
**Pain:** Multiple trades, inconsistent bid formats, delays.
**Solution:** Create RFQs per package; enable partial bids; compare; negotiate in chat; award; auto-PO.
**Proof Points:** Versioned RFQs; audit trail; PO PDF; supplier performance visibility.
**KPIs:** RFQ cycle time ↓, variance to budget ↓, award lead time ↓.

## 2) Quantity Surveyor: Advisory & Reporting
**Audience:** QS firms/consultancies.
**Pain:** Manual normalization; scattered emails.
**Solution:** Side-by-side comparisons; supplier scorecards; draft/save/edit RFQs; exportable summaries.
**KPIs:** Reporting time ↓, client satisfaction ↑, rework ↓.

## 3) Developer/Owner: Risk-Managed Sourcing
**Audience:** Client-side PMO, owner’s rep.
**Pain:** Single-vendor dependency; opaque pricing.
**Solution:** Closed vs open RFQs; partial awards; live updates; PO tracking.
**KPIs:** Supplier concentration risk ↓, overruns ↓.

## 4) SME Supplier: Win & Operate
**Audience:** Small/medium manufacturers/distributors.
**Pain:** Low discovery; admin overhead.
**Solution:** Category RFQs; reusable T&C templates; public product catalog with inquiry.
**KPIs:** Invitations ↑, win rate ↑, PO acceptance time ↓.

## 5) Enterprise Procurement: Scale & Compliance
**Audience:** Large multi-site firms.
**Pain:** Volume and governance.
**Solution:** CSV/API import; authorized supplier lists; audit logs; exportable POs.
**KPIs:** Throughput ↑, exceptions ↓, audit findings ↓.
